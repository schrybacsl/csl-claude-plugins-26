# Go/No-Go Memo

Two pages, written before any drafting hour is spent, initialed by the decision owner. Number `CSL-<PROG>-MEM-<yymmdd>` in the pursuit folder (`csl-document-control`). Method in `csl-bd-pursuits` Section 3.3; the 10-business-day escalation rule in `references/escalation-matrix.md`. A criterion without evidence is "not established (score withheld)", never a guess. Higher-education pursuits use the weighted matrix in the installed `higher-ed-bidding` skill in place of the score table below.

---

**CSL-<PROG>-MEM-<yymmdd>** | Go/no-go: [solicitation number and title] | Prepared by [capture lead] for [responsible PX/Director] and the Marketing Manager | Day MM/DD/YY | Time zone [ET / CT]

## 1. Recommendation (bottom line)

One sentence: Go / Conditional go / No-go. Then the conditions, each with an owner and a date.

## 2. Solicitation summary

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Owner / agency | Legal name of the owner or agency, and the department issuing |
| Solicitation | Number, title, project number |
| Role sought | As the solicitation names it |
| Delivery method | DBB / CM at Risk / CM-Build / Design-Build / services only |
| Program value or size | As published; "not stated" if not |
| Term | Contract term or project duration, with option years |
| Pre-submittal | Date, time, zone, location; mandatory or not |
| Questions deadline | Date, time, zone |
| Due | Date, day of week, time, zone; method (portal / hard copies / email) |
| Fee requested at this stage | Yes / no / separate envelope |
| Gates | Each with lead time and status |

## 3. Fit score table

| Criterion | Score (1 to 5) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sector | 1 to 5, or withheld | Projects in `csl-projects` that match |
| Scope | 1 to 5, or withheld | Role CSL performs; anything physical or contract-holding is a mismatch |
| Geography | 1 to 5, or withheld | Staffing, travel, local presence |
| Size | 1 to 5, or withheld | Published value against CSL's comparable projects |
| Incumbency and competition | 1 to 5, or withheld | Incumbent named; firms expected; CSL relationship with the owner |
| Prequal and registration gates | 1 to 5; 1 if a gate cannot clear | Each gate and whether it clears inside the runway |
| Deadline runway | 1 to 5 | Calendar and business days from this memo to the due date |
| Team availability | 1 to 5, or withheld | Named candidates and their current allocations |
| Price and fee posture | 1 to 5, or withheld | Fee form; QBS or price-scored; CSL rate posture |
| Strategic value | 1 to 5 | New client, sector, geography, or vehicle |

Reading (package convention): any gate that cannot clear inside the runway is a no-go on its own; otherwise 35 or more of 50 on scored criteria is go, 25 to 34 conditional go with named conditions, below 25 no-go. State the number of criteria scored and withheld.

## 4. Resourcing ask

Capture lead; writers by section; reviewer for the red team; Marketing production window; proposed team members to confirm; estimated hours by role for the response.

## 6. Decision owner

Responsible PX or Director for the go; Marketing Manager for production; Partners for strategic pursuits (new market, new sector, fee above the PX threshold). Initials and date on the issued copy.
