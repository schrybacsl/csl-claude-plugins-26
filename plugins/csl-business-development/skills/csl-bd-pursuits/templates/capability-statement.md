## Differentiators (three, each with proof)

1. **Owner's advocate across the whole capital lifecycle.** Governance, budget and schedule controls, risk, procurement support, oversight, commissioning and turnover, on programs from a single renovation to multi-building campuses. Proof: Owner's Representative on Cytiva Project Falcon (277,438 SF; governance development through closeout oversight); Owner's Project Manager on Harvard Business School Klarman Hall.
2. **Controls and reporting the owner can act on.** Weekly tri-edition executive reporting (Look-Ahead, Mid-Week, End-of-Week), live dashboards fed from the program's own trackers, numbered deliverables with transmittal evidence, and asset data migrated into the owner's maintenance system before turnover.
3. **Live-environment delivery.** Phased work in occupied and 24-hour facilities with mitigation and communications staff on the CSL team. Proof: Tufts Medical Center CPD Central Sterile multi-phase equipment replacement in a 24-hour active facility; Tufts Medical Center 4-Phase GI Endoscopy renovation of an active clinic; Buckingham Browne & Nichols Middle School relocation management and neighbor relations.
