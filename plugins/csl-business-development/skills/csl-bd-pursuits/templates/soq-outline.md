# SOQ / RFQ Response Outline

Section-by-section outline for a Statement of Qualifications or RFQ response, used when the solicitation does not prescribe its own tab order (when it does, follow the solicitation and map these sections onto it in the compliance matrix). Number `CSL-<PROG>-SOQ-<solicitation no.>`; drafts in Word via `skills/csl-brand/assets/docx_styles.py`; Marketing (N. Graboyes) owns layout, graphics, references, and photos per `csl-brand`. Method in `csl-bd-pursuits` Sections 3.5 to 3.8. Page budgets below assume a 30-page limit exclusive of forms; scale to the solicitation's limit and count pages the way the owner counts them.

---

## Front matter (not counted unless the solicitation counts it)

| Element | Content | Supplier |
|---|---|---|
| Cover | Solicitation number and title; owner; CSL Consulting; date; logo per `csl-brand` Section 3; client mark only if the solicitation permits | Marketing |
| Transmittal / cover letter (1 page) | Addressed to the named procurement contact; project and solicitation number in the first line; the one differentiator for this owner; the proposed lead by name; the commitment (availability, office of record); signed by a Partner or Vice President | Agent drafts; Partner signs |
| Table of contents | Mirrors the solicitation's evaluation criteria headings | Marketing |

## Tab 1. Firm profile (2 pages)

CSL Consulting, LLC, 152 Middlesex Turnpike, Burlington, MA 01803; years in practice and staff count from Marketing's current figures; ownership and legal form as the CFO states them; sectors served with one named project each from `csl-projects`; role definitions in the owner's vocabulary (owner's representative, owner's project manager, program controls, tenant representation, mitigation and communications); office that will serve the contract. No superlatives.

## Tab 2. Understanding of the project (2 pages)

What the owner published, restated in the owner's words with the source; the three things that will decide success on this project (from the pre-submittal memo and the solicitation's emphasis); what CSL will do about each in one sentence, pointing forward to the approach tab.

## Tab 3. Relevant experience (6 to 10 pages)

Three to eight projects selected per `csl-bd-pursuits` 3.6 and `references/qualification-selection.md`. One page or half page each:

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Project name | Exact name from `csl-projects` |
| Client and location | As recorded |
| Size and value | Only figures recorded in `csl-projects`; value from Marketing when the solicitation requires it |
| Scope | Two to four sentences from the record; cleanroom class, phasing, 24-hour operation, relocation where present |
| CSL role and services | As recorded (owner's representative; tenant representation; full project management; OPM) |
| Dates | From Marketing's project file |
| Reference contact | Name, title, phone, email supplied by Marketing after confirming the reference is willing |
| Relevance to this solicitation | One sentence tying the project to a scored criterion |
| Photo | Marketing library; captioned |

Example entry (attributes from `csl-projects`; dates, value, and reference from Marketing):

> **Cytiva, Project Falcon.** Duncan, South Carolina. 277,438 SF construction and fit-out of a manufacturing facility for single-use and filtration technologies: administrative, training, production, and support areas; production suites ISO-7 in operation and ISO-5 flexible manufacturing; ISO-8 and ISO-7 gowning, kitting, and preparation; 60,000 SF ISO-classified in total; over 10 MW of redundant power with critical electrical switchgear; 2,050+ tons of cooling capacity. CSL role: Owner's Representative, including governance development, RFP review, weekly OAC meetings, site visits and field reports, CM requisition review, schedule review, risk log management, change order review, project controls, executive reporting, and closeout oversight. Relevance: high-utility-load manufacturing with cleanroom classification and a full controls scope.

## Tab 4. Proposed team (4 to 6 pages)

Org chart (one page: owner at top, CSL roles, A/E and contractor as coordinated parties); role descriptions; one-page resumes in Marketing's template (name, directory title per `csl-people`, role on this pursuit, years, relevant projects with role and one line each, education and registrations as supplied by the person); availability statement by phase with percent allocation and office of record.

## Tab 5. Approach (6 to 8 pages)

Phases (pre-design or planning, design, procurement, construction, commissioning and turnover, closeout), then the blocks from `csl-bd-pursuits` 3.7 that the solicitation scores, each with its proof line: governance; budget and cost controls; schedule controls; risk management; procurement support; design and construction oversight; permits and safety coordination; commissioning and turnover; reporting cadence (weekly tri-edition executive reporting); dashboards; document control; mitigation and communications; relocation and equipment planning. Close with the first 30 days as a dated list and the transition plan at the end of the contract.

## Tab 6. Schedule and availability (1 page)

Mobilization date from notice to proceed; staffing curve by phase; commitments of key staff to other assignments stated as percentages; the office of record and travel plan.

## Tab 7. Fee (only when requested; separate volume or sealed envelope when the solicitation says so)

Rates by role per the CSL rate table; assumptions; exclusions; reimbursables; term. Prepared with the CFO and the PX per `csl-cost-change-control`; never bound into the technical volume unless the solicitation directs it.

## Tab 8. Required forms

Every form listed in the compliance matrix, in the solicitation's order, signed by the named signatory; addenda acknowledgment; non-collusion and debarment certifications; insurance letter; licenses; W-9 or equivalent as requested.

## Appendices

Certifications and registrations as Marketing holds them; project photos; letters of reference when permitted; the compliance matrix when the solicitation asks for a cross-reference.
