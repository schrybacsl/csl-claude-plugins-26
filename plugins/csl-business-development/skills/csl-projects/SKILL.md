## 1. ADVANCED MANUFACTURING

### Cytiva — Project Falcon
- **Location:** Duncan, South Carolina
- **Size:** 277,438 SF
- **Scope:** Construction and fitout of manufacturing facility for single-use and filtration technologies. Includes administrative, training, production, and support areas. Production suites are ISO-7 in operation / ISO-5 flexible manufacturing. ISO-8 & ISO-7 gowning, kitting, and preparation areas. Total ISO-classified area: 60,000 SF. Over 10MW of redundant power, critical electrical switchgear, 2,050+ tons of cooling capacity.
- **CSL Role:** Owner's Representative — governance development, RFP review, weekly OAC meetings, site visits/field reports, CM requisition review, schedule review, risk log management, change order review, project controls, executive reporting, closeout oversight.
- **URL:** https://www.csl-consulting.com/projects/advancedmanufacturing/cytiva-project-falcon/

### ErlingKlinger — New Production Facility
- **Location:** Easley, South Carolina
- **Size:** 245,000 SF (2 new buildings)
- **Scope:** First American EV Battery production plant — Phase 1. 210,000 SF battery production (electrode manufacturing, cell assembly, formation & aging, module/pack assembly, QC) + 35,000 SF R&D lab and office. ISO-4 and ISO-7 cleanroom space. High-capacity electrical supply (fully electrified building). Specialized fire suppression.
- **CSL Role:** Tenant Representation — project controls, risk management, financial analysis, executive reporting, oversight of design/build contractor for tenant fitout, owner's interests for landlord's core/shell design/construction, permitting support with local and state authorities.
- **URL:** https://www.csl-consulting.com/projects/advancedmanufacturing/erlingklinger-new-production-facility/
