# Signature Block

Plain text. No logo image, no brand colors in the text, no quotations, no banners (`csl-brand` Section 5, Email). Title per `csl-people`; the engagement role follows a vertical bar when the thread is client-facing. Build every block from the operator's Microsoft 365 profile (`get_me`: display name, title, email) and the engagement context file (engagement line); never from another employee's block. The mobile number comes from the operator's own Outlook signature and is not recorded in this package. Office address: use the operator's office from the directory; the Burlington address below is one example office. Company form on the legal line is `CSL Consulting, LLC` (`csl-brand` Section 4).

## Other CSL staff operating the agent

Swap name, title, and email from `csl-people` (email pattern `firstinitiallastname@csl-consulting.com`); keep the engagement line to the role the engagement defines for them. Title is the directory title, not a self-description.

## Rules

- One signature per email; replies inside a thread may carry the short form.
- Time zone is not part of the signature; it belongs beside each time in the body.
- Confidentiality line, when the audience is external to the client team, is the single sentence in `csl-brand` Section 4, placed after the signature, not before.
- Never a personal social link or slogan; never a quotation.
- Never "CSL LLC", "CSL Consulting LLC" without the comma, or "C.S.L.".
