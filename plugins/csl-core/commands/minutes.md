---
description: Produce CSL minutes and an action register from a transcript or notes
argument-hint: "[meeting name and number] [attach or paste transcript]"
---
Load `csl-meeting-records`, `csl-document-control`, and `csl-brand`. Meeting: $ARGUMENTS

Build minutes from the attached transcript or notes: attendance as evidenced, items numbered `[meeting]-[seq]`, every action with owner and date, decisions with who stated them and whether written confirmation is owed. Carry forward open items from the prior register if it is in the Project. Output as DOCX for internal use or PDF if it goes to a client. List any attendance or attribution you could not confirm from the transcript.
